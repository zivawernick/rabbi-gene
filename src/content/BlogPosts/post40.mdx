Dear Friends,

I thought I would share with you a short paper I have written:

I am teaching a zoom class on Talmud on the third chapter of Bava Metzia.

The first word of the Mishnah "HaMafkid" (Who deposits) set my thoughts on fire because the reading for the Torah on first day Rosh Hashanah is: "God visited Sarah" meaning the birth of Isaac. The verb "to visit" is in the causative meaning to make a deposit!

So:

The birth of Jesus as an intercession of God is regarded as singular with perhaps comparison with birth of heroes in the ancient world by gods inseminating human women.

What is however overlooked is a passage in Genesis recording the birth of Isaac that could have been an influence upon the later account of the birth of Jesus.

A comparison of these two events begs the question of possible influence.

Genesis 18:9-14

<p dir="rtl">וַיֹּאמְר֣וּ אֵׄלָ֔יׄוׄ אַיֵּ֖ה שָׂרָ֣ה אִשְׁתֶּ֑ךָ וַיֹּ֖אמֶר הִנֵּ֥ה בָאֹֽהֶל׃</p>

They said to him, "Where is your wife Sarah?" And he replied, "There, in the tent."

<p dir="rtl">וַיֹּ֗אמֶר שׁ֣וֹב אָשׁ֤וּב אֵלֶ֙יךָ֙ כָּעֵ֣ת חַיָּ֔ה וְהִנֵּה־בֵ֖ן לְשָׂרָ֣ה אִשְׁתֶּ֑ךָ וְשָׂרָ֥ה שֹׁמַ֛עַת פֶּ֥תַח הָאֹ֖הֶל וְה֥וּא אַחֲרָֽיו׃</p>

Then one said, "I will return to you next year, and your wife Sarah shall have a son!" Sarah was listening at the entrance of the tent, which was behind him.

<p dir="rtl">וְאַבְרָהָ֤ם וְשָׂרָה֙ זְקֵנִ֔ים בָּאִ֖ים בַּיָּמִ֑ים חָדַל֙ לִהְי֣וֹת לְשָׂרָ֔ה אֹ֖רַח כַּנָּשִֽׁים׃</p>

Now Abraham and Sarah were old, advanced in years; Sarah had stopped having her periods.

<p dir="rtl">וַתִּצְחַ֥ק שָׂרָ֖ה בְּקִרְבָּ֣הּ לֵאמֹ֑ר אַחֲרֵ֤י בְלֹתִי֙ הָֽיְתָה־לִּ֣י עֶדְנָ֔ה וַֽאדֹנִ֖י זָקֵֽן׃</p>

And Sarah laughed to herself, saying, "Now that I've lost the ability, am I to have enjoyment—with my husband so old?"

<p dir="rtl">וַיֹּ֥אמֶר יְהֹוָ֖ה אֶל־אַבְרָהָ֑ם לָ֣מָּה זֶּה֩ צָחֲקָ֨ה שָׂרָ֜ה לֵאמֹ֗ר הַאַ֥ף אֻמְנָ֛ם אֵלֵ֖ד וַאֲנִ֥י זָקַֽנְתִּי׃</p>

Then GOD said to Abraham, "Why did Sarah laugh, saying, 'Shall I in truth bear a child, old as I am?'

<p dir="rtl">הֲיִפָּלֵ֥א מֵיְהֹוָ֖ה דָּבָ֑ר לַמּוֹעֵ֞ד אָשׁ֥וּב אֵלֶ֛יךָ כָּעֵ֥ת חַיָּ֖ה וּלְשָׂרָ֥ה בֵֽן׃</p>

Is anything too wondrous for GOD? I will return to you at the same season next year, and Sarah shall have a son."

Genesis 21:1-2

<p dir="rtl">וַֽיהֹוָ֛ה פָּקַ֥ד אֶת־שָׂרָ֖ה כַּאֲשֶׁ֣ר אָמָ֑ר וַיַּ֧עַשׂ יְהֹוָ֛ה לְשָׂרָ֖ה כַּאֲשֶׁ֥ר דִּבֵּֽר׃</p>

GOD took note of Sarah just as promised, and GOD did for Sarah what had been announced.

<p dir="rtl">וַתַּ֩הַר֩ וַתֵּ֨לֶד שָׂרָ֧ה לְאַבְרָהָ֛ם בֵּ֖ן לִזְקֻנָ֑יו לַמּוֹעֵ֕ד אֲשֶׁר־דִּבֶּ֥ר אֹת֖וֹ אֱלֹהִֽים׃</p>

Sarah conceived and bore a son to Abraham in his old age, at the set time of which God had spoken.

In the verse "God took note of Sarah," the verb is unusual in Hebrew: "PKD." In the causative form this verb is used to make a "Deposit"!

Clearly the birth of a son to a woman several years past menopause is as miraculous as the birth of a son from a virgin! Notice the "annunciation" is found in both stories that emphasizes the parallel.

Luke 1:26-38

**The Birth of Jesus Foretold**

<sup>26</sup> In the sixth month of Elizabeth's pregnancy, God sent the angel Gabriel to Nazareth, a town in Galilee, <sup>27</sup> to a virgin pledged to be married to a man named Joseph, a descendant of David. The virgin's name was Mary. <sup>28</sup> The angel went to her and said, "Greetings, you who are highly favored! The Lord is with you."

<sup>29</sup> Mary was greatly troubled at his words and wondered what kind of greeting this might be. <sup>30</sup> But the angel said to her, "Do not be afraid, Mary; you have found favor with God. <sup>31</sup> You will conceive and give birth to a son, and you are to call him Jesus. <sup>32</sup> He will be great and will be called the Son of the Most High. The Lord God will give him the throne of his father David, <sup>33</sup> and he will reign over Jacob's descendants forever; his kingdom will never end."

<sup>34</sup> "How will this be," Mary asked the angel, "since I am a virgin?"

<sup>35</sup> The angel answered, "The Holy Spirit will come on you, and the power of the Most High will overshadow you. So the holy one to be born will be called the Son of God. <sup>36</sup> Even Elizabeth your relative is going to have a child in her old age, and she who was said to be unable to conceive is in her sixth month. <sup>37</sup> For no word from God will ever fail."

<sup>38</sup> "I am the Lord's servant," Mary answered. "May your word to me be fulfilled." Then the angel left her.

**Matthew 1:18-20**

Now the birth of Jesus Christ took place in this way. When his mother Mary had been betrothed to Joseph, before they came together she was found to be with child from the Holy Spirit. And her husband Joseph, being a just man and unwilling to put her to shame, resolved to divorce her quietly. But as he considered these things, behold, an angel of the Lord appeared to him in a dream, saying, "Joseph, son of David, do not fear to take Mary as your wife, for that which is conceived in her is from the Holy Spirit."

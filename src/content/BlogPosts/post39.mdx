Dear all,

I have tried to keep up with posts about current events even though my wife and I have been plagued by a bout of ill health recently.

I would like to wish everyone a Shabbat Shalom. We all need at least a respite from events around the world and close to home that are unsettling and cause for worry.

But I must take a moment to comment on the botched execution that resulted in the almost farcical successful attempt of saving the life of the condemned after they tried to kill her.

Executions are murder committed with our approval by the agencies of government that conduct them. They speak to a past age of barbarism. Hopefully in every successive age our moral sensibilities improve. The rabbis tried to mute the horrific punishments of the Bible and the greatest rabbinic sage Moses Maimonides in his code limited the death penalty to homicide. The rabbis had previously attenuated the possibility of such a punishment by demanding that the murderer accept his punishment before committing the act before two witnesses.

The proposal to video a firing squad execution is a gross descent into indecency.

I know that families of victims may wish for revenge. In former times they were given carte blanche to exact punishment on the offenders. But we have lived through so many wars and so many killings of innocents as well as the lives of soldiers on every side who also represent human life. Can we not reach some stage of ethical and moral development in which the killing of other human beings is banned from our society? For those who may believe that humans are created in the image of God then destroying that image should be considered sacrilege!

Enough of the lies that promote hatred and distrust. Enough of the desire for that which belongs to others to be the rationale for robbery, rape and murder.

We are capable left to our own devices to destroy the beautiful world in which we have been gifted. Can reason defeat the insanity that drives humanity to destruction? That is the question!

For Jews we begin again the reading of the Book of Creation. After the first six days, the Creator saw all that He created and it was good. There was no violence and yet there was rest.

May this coming Sabbath bring at least peace to you all and a prayer for peace for a world in need of peace.

Shabbat Shalom

Love

gene

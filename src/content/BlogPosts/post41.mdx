More than several years ago I gave up fishing. I realized that the "fight" on the line I was enjoying was the death throes of a dying fish. I had as a young man once picked up a BB gun and aimed at a flying bird, but I fortunately missed and never repeated that action. Not to paint myself as a saint but the realization that taking the life of any creature other than for self-defense is immoral according to the standards of action I would wish for myself and others.

That is why I must again cry out against the death penalty and especially the videoing of it.

I call to task the spiritual leaders of this country no matter their denomination or faith to voice outrage at this desecration of the sacredness of life.

What use the prayers and rituals if they are not the product and impetus for a standard of morality that this world so sorely needs?!!!!

How soon do we forget that with all the rituals of the Day of Atonement (the most sacred day of the Jewish year) with all the rituals of the High Priest, with all the confession of sins, with all the pleas for forgiveness, the rabbis placed after the Torah reading the words of the prophet Isaiah:

> Is such the fast I desire? A day for men to starve their bodies? Is it bowing the head like a bulrush and lying in sackcloth and ashes? Do you call that a fast? A day when the Lord is favorable?
>
> No, this is the fast I desire:\
> To unlock the fetters of wickedness and untie the cords of the yoke.\
> To let the oppressed go free\
> To break off every yoke\
> It is to share your bread with the hungry,\
> And to take the wretched poor into your home.\
> When you see the naked, to clothe him,\
> And not to ignore your own kin.\
> Then shall your light burst through like the dawn\
> And your healing spring up quickly;\
> Your Vindicator shall march before you,\
> The Presence of the LORD shall be your rear guard.

(Isaiah 58:5–8)

How fast are these words forgotten!!!

Are not these words engraved into the moral tenets of Christianity and Islam and in the very innards of Rabbinic Judaism?!

Why the silence? If religion in its formal adherence has lost membership in the civilized world it is because besides those voices promising individual salvation to be given a ticket to some form of eternal life, we are missing the voice of righteousness crying out in the wilderness, the jungle in which we live of hate, murder and deprivation.

Where is the care for the poor, those who are deficient in their abilities either of mind or body?

Where is the care for the earth that gives its riches to us in abundance and asks only that we guarantee that process by caring for that from which we receive such goodness?

At the end of my rabbinical career, I am desolate that those values I cherished and sought in all my ways to inculcate in both my own community and at large are no longer cherished even by those who claim the mantle of faith.

A public outcry against the video of an execution must be the first step. Then a campaign against the death penalty for even the most heinous crimes, for whom should we designate to commit murder in our name? For that is what an execution is. By definition: murder is the killing of someone who cannot harm you and an executioner is a murderer in our name!

Isaiah was a voice crying in the wilderness of an immoral society. Can we hear his voice? Can we cry out in his name?

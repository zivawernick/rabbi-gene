You can be assured that you can even find Jewish members of your political ilk who for reasons of promoting themselves within your ranks will come to your defense.

Antisemitism or as it is now called antizionism (as if the denial of the Jews of all people to have a national homeland is not antisemitism by a different nomenclature) is only evil when it is espoused by someone of a different political view.

Jews who are supposedly so crafty, underhanded, wicked and capable of any and all evil are duped into turning a blind or forgiving eye to whatever political party they choose as the leaders thereof make peace and accept within their broadest tent possible pronounced antisemites who by their words, actions and slogans harbor nothing but hatred towards the sons and daughters of Abraham and Sarah.

While hundreds of millions of Christians and Moslems can live in hundreds of countries that proclaim their dominant religion to be the foundation of their national state the infinitesimal number of Jews much less than ten million are to be denied their own homeland.

And in the mystery of the terrible pains that some Jews try as they may to appease those who only wish them ill as they bend them selves backwards and attempt double somersaults only to find that their purity in denouncing Israel, Zionism and Jewish community can never meet the standard of those who despise Zion and its flock.

It is time for Jews to stand up! Let the world know that there would be no Christianity no Islam if the Jew Moses did not come down from the mountain with two tablets of stone! There would be no Christianity no Islam if a Jew Jesus did not preach in Jewish Palestine! There would be no Islam if the stories of Abraham were not recited in the desert of Arabia!

If you are an antisemite, you are against the people and their ideals that gave the standard of morality to the world. If you are an antisemite your goal is to destroy the people so you can destroy the values that they have projected onto the world stage.

And to those who are ill-informed, for those who cannot reason and for those are complacent know full well history has taught us that it never stops with the Jews.

They may be the first, but you will be the last. Just ask Pastor Martin Niemoller who experienced too late what is in store for all those who passively allow antisemitism to fester!

Dear All,

I am more than annoyed by the recent trend in news and information articles to judge everything by length.  As if the length of an article gives it stature and intellectual heft.

More often the truth is obfuscated by a deluge of verbiage that conceals truth behind a mass of disinformation.

The greatest moral statements were simple and direct.

Hillel’s famous: “What is hateful to you do not do to another; the rest is commentary go study” is direct and to the point.

The most powerful statement of moral accountability in the Bible is the book of Amos.  Amos wastes no time bringing the society of his time to account.

“You alone have I known from all the families of the earth; therefor I will hold you to responsible for all your sins.”

If you believe yourself to be a spiritual moral person, then you must hold yourself to the highest standard of righteous action.  No ifs, no buts no excuses.

Forgive me but spare me the protestations of so many “religious” of every denomination and cult.  Your voices of devotion to your creeds are not matched by your care in living moral, ethical lives devoted to helping the poor, the mental challenged, the uneducated and those who live in the darkness of despair.

There is no war that is more of a devilish impulse than a command of the highest source of morality and spirituality.

The common good of this biosphere means the common good for all that is contained herein.

A simple story to sum it all up.

A student tests his teacher.  I have a bird in my hand is it alive or dead?  The wise teacher knows that if he says it is alive the student will crush it.  If he says it is dead the student will release it to fly away.

The wise teacher replies: “The answer is in your hand!”

So, for all of us as to the future:

The answer is in our hands!

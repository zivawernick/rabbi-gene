Dear Friends,

As I am planning to go for Rosh Hashanah to my son for the Holiday, I thought I would give you all my Shabbat and New Year greetings and blessings for peace, health and achievement.

Of course, I have the temerity to share with you the musings of my mind. I confess to playing on my phone in so many spare moments, chess. I am troubled by advertisements that popup and provoke anger. Especially I noted this app for "Kalshi" that seems to be a betting app. There seems to be too many opportunities for people, especially young men to bet online on practically everything but especially sports.

Anyone with any knowledge knows that the possibility of winning is slight, but the lure of the big hit entices people to waste their money hoping for the big win. This applies to state gambling that is so popular with so many opportunities to waste your money chasing the big hit!

The fact that our governments use these gambling offerings to raise money is an indirect tax on citizens of usually moderate means. Unfortunately, this has replaced other forms of gambling that preyed on those of lesser means.

Is it righteous for the government in our name to raise funds by such schemes?

I know that paying taxes is something most people object to but submit unwillingly. Would it not be a righteous thing for those who have the means to not object but encourage the various governments to raise taxes to support all the worthwhile projects that only the federal and state governments can accomplish.

One of the ways in which people of wealth could demean the appeal of the "Democratic Socialists" is by evincing a patriotic conviction that paying taxes for the welfare of all is a noble and desirable activity. "Zedakah" in the original meaning in Hebrew is "Justice."

Helping others achieves for us the double reward of the person who receives and the sense of righteousness that the benefactor feels.

When we think of what the New Year will bring for us, let us pray that it brings the rewards of a community that is united in purpose to lift up every member thereof so all can benefit from the abundant blessings that God and nature may bestow upon us.

Shabbat Shalom, may you be written in the Book of Life for the coming year for health and all goodness.

Gene

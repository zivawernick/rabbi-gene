"What is crooked cannot be straightened." Ecclesiastes 1:15

President Obama in a speech that he gave accepting an honorary degree in Belfast made gratuitous remarks that perhaps were his cover for going to Michigan to support El Sayed for Senate.

First, there was no reason for him to mention the Israel/Palestine issue in Belfast.

And then he proceeded with such an ignorance of the development of the Yishuv in Israel before independence that it begs the question whether his falsification of the details of Jewish rights to the land of Israel was not done intentionally. Certainly, Obama is no lightweight intellectually. Whenever he spoke in the past about any issue it was with the full grasp of the history that preceded the current events.

He gave the impression that Israel was founded by refugees from the Holocaust who lost land and lives and then proceeded to take land from the Arab population.

There is no way that he can apologize for such a misstatement of fact.

Let us review the history.

There always was a Jewish presence in Israel. In Jerusalem there were always more Jews than Christians or Moslems. In Tiberias, Safed and Hebron the Jewish communities lived from ancient times.

In the last part of the nineteenth century Jewish pioneers from Czarist Russia established agricultural communities on land bought from absentee owners in Syria and other places.

They drained swamps and brought the desert to bloom. The British Peel report noted that there was an unimpeded Arab immigration into Palestine attracted by the Jewish development that offered them employment.

And yes, state it very clearly: There would have been no Nakba if five different Arab nations had not invaded the land ceded to the Jews by the United Nations in 1947. And may we add that they were welcomed by the Arabs in Palestine with the desire to kill the Jews and push them into the sea. For this does President Obama feel regret?

Off he goes to campaign for El Sayed who welcomed Hasan Piker when he was in the primary. The very same who said America deserved 9/11.

There are Jews whose allegiance to the Democrat party "trumps" their own very self-interest. How can any Jew vote for a man who cannot tell the difference between Hamas and Israel? For that matter how can any thinking person vote for him?

No matter which way he may choose to squirm Obama cannot apologize for the damage done.

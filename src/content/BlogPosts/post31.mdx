Dear Friends,

Shabbat Shalom!

Antisemitism is an evil that stalks the western world. It has been engraved into the culture since ancient times. It was fueled and encouraged by the growth of Christianity and Islam and although denied validity by more modern expressions of those religions it still is baked into the mindset of too many of their adherents.

The war against antisemitism has been taken up by good people both Jewish and others. The attention to any manifestation of it is necessary.

That being said there are times that Jews and others who are in the frontlines of the fight against this evil should step back and consider where and when to battle.

The future Met Gala honoring John Galliano is a point in question. Art and Literature are expressions of human creativity. Once they are fashioned and accepted for their intrinsic worth, they no longer reflect the views of those who created them but belong to the culture that for the most part has shaped the Western World.

It really matters little if Galliano has made amends for his past outbursts. He is not being honored for who he was. It is the achievement that he produced as a fashion designer that has intrinsic worth in the eyes of those who judge fashion.

We listen and look at the works of Richard Wagner on Opera Stage. We read the poetry of Ezra Pound and T.S. Eliot, children are enchanted by the works of Roald Dahl.

How foolish it would be to deprive ourselves of that which has intrinsic merit because of the defects of the creators thereof.

The better way is to deny the antisemites to be the arbiters of what we deem of worth. Creativity is the highest achievement of the human mind and spirit. Hatred is the foulest fault to which humanity can fall. We raise ourselves when we acknowledge the good and the beautiful. As for the creators thereof they must come hopefully to a reckoning with that that diminishes them while their creations are accepted on their intrinsic worth.

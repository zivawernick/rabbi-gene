Dear all,

The worst sin that any Jew can do does not have a punishment by any human judicial court at this moment in time. That is the sin of insulting the name of God by deeds that cast aspersion on what we as Jews hold most dear.

You will notice that if a Jew picks up a prayer book one will often kiss it. When the Torah is brought in procession we kiss it.

But there are actions that bring all that we hold sacred into disrepute.

Yehudah "The Pious" (Jehudah ben Samuel d. 1217 Regensburg Germany), was the author of the book "Sefer Hasidim-Book of the Pious."

He states explicitly as many other moralists have done that if a Jew steals from another Jew, it is theft but if he steals from a non-Jew it is a "profanation of the name of God," because the non-Jew will think that that is what Jews are allowed to do!

I remember once when a Jew made the papers for a crime a minister said to me: "I thought your people were above that!"

**Now to the point!** The burning of mosques in the West Bank by Jewish hooligans must be punished. There is no excuse for the Israeli government to let these villains run wild. For too long the Israeli authorities have not only restrained but in most cases abetted the actions of these terrorists. They have burned crops, wounded and killed innocent Arabs. The inaction of the Israeli government to restrain and punish these hooligans gives fodder to the claim of "genocide" in Gaza.

Once again, the so called "religious authorities" of the ultra-orthodox in Israel are more interested in keeping their loafers out of the IDF and milking the coffers of the government for their empires of ignorance than in addressing moral concerns.

A café open on Shabbat in Jerusalem is against the keeping of Shabbat. That is a matter to be adjudicated between the owners and the Almighty!

There is a difference in Halacha between actions that are between persons and ritual acts that are to be adjudicated by the "Heavenly Court." Actions that are between one person and another that are to be adjudicated by the human legal system that should prevent harm and punish those who offend.

When such actions not only do harm but also tarnish the "Name of God" they demand swift action to remedy the infraction.

To Benjamin Netanyahu I would say: Protecting the "Name of God" and the moral integrity of the State of Israel is more important than holding onto a government that includes those who abet and incite actions that defame the name of God and the Jewish people.

I make no bones about it I am interested in Jewish survival. I was a child during World War Two and came of age when the knowledge of the Holocaust became evident.

Two incidents shaped my understanding of the situation of Jewish life. Number one in Hebrew school our teacher who was at Nuremberg showed us pictures of the bodies piled up at Auschwitz or some other concentration camp. I doubt if he could do that today. All those naked emaciated bodies shown to preteen children!

Number two. I was a blonde-haired blue-eyed boy that could have been a poster child for Hitler Youth. I was standing in a storefront looking in the window when a Gentile couple came up to me to commiserate with me telling me how bad the Jews were!

From that time on I devoted my life to the preservation of the Jewish people. I became a rabbi to ensure that the Jews survived both physically and spiritually.

I have studied all the texts and mastered the history as few have accomplished.

My devotion to Israel is based on the simple fact that it is the only country where a Jew will not be killed because they are Jewish! The reason that there is no Palestinian State is that hitherto the Palestinians have not convinced the Israelis that they do not wish to kill them. On October the Seventh it should be noted that Hamas killed not only Jews but Moslems and Asians. Note well even though Moslems in Israel suffer certain disabilities there is no exodus to any of the Arab countries in the area. Arab doctors are not leaving Israel. Twenty percent of the students at the Technion (Israel's MIT) are Arab.

Yes, I vigorously protest against the current Israeli government. I have former students that are working for a better future for Jews and Arabs in Israel.

Let us examine Jon Stewart's credentials for criticizing Senator John Fetterman. Stewart is a Jew by birth is that his credential. Does he have a Jewish home? When was the last time he was in a synagogue? Has he ever contributed to a Jewish charity? Is Jon Stewart an authority on Jewish history? Is he aware that the United States Armed forces have a deep and profitable connection with the IDF.

No matter what one thinks of the war with Iran, ask any military expert never in the history of warfare was such a close and successful cooperation was accomplished by two allies as that between the IDF and the armed forces of the United States.

It is not the first time and most probably not the last time that in a situation where Jewish lives are threatened there are Jews who wish to curry favor with those who wish the Jewish people ill will.

I remember seeing a photograph of Hitler and cohorts in the early days before he came to power. In the photograph there is a blank space between two individuals. At the bottom of the blank space there is a pair of shoes. The missing person is a Jew who ingratiated himself into the coterie of Hitler. All that remains of him is a pair of shoes.

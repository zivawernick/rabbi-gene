Isaiah 36:6

Look, I know you are depending on Egypt, that splintered reed of a staff, which pierces the hand of anyone who leans on it!

Woe to Ben Gevir and company and the silent suffering Netanyahu.

So much for those who place their trust in ---------

And after alienating practically everyone in the civilized world with their vengeful words and incitement of those who have committed atrocities, they have the stupidity to criticize publicly the only sometime “friend” they have.  And the shame of being lectured by JD Vance.  O how the mighty are fallen!

It is time for Teshuvah!  The way of war is destruction, but the way of peace is construction.

Israel should commit to rebuilding Gaza.  An appeal to the Arab nations to join in a rebuilding plan for the Gaza strip concurrent with the disarmament of Hamas.

A commitment to a Palestinian State to be established in what is now the West Bank with borders to be decided in the future with mutual consent.

Wars with bullets decides only death; Peace with understanding preserves life.

It is time to wage peace!

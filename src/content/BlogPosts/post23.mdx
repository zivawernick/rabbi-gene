Dear Friends,

A few thoughts on the approaching celebration of July fourth.

The study of history is like the five blind men with the elephant. The one holding the trunk thinks it is a hose, the tail is a snake, the side a wall, the leg a column, the tusk neither this nor that.

There are those who will separate the north from the south, those who will emphasize the ideas of enlightenment, and those who will insist on the perpetual stain of slavery and the ensuing racial prejudice that has engulfed us to this day.

However, I am reminded of a quote attributed to Edmund Burke that both the Puritans of New England and the Cavaliers of the South came from the same poor English stock, but the southerners thought of themselves as descended from nobility and thus were much happier.

A word to the issue of Black Slavery that has defined the struggle for decency and appreciation of each and everyone on the merits of their accomplishments.

First let us establish that all are guilty.

The Blacks that were sold into slavery were captives of Black tribes in Africa. In the inter-tribal warfare victorious tribes found it was more profitable not to kill their defeated rivals but sell them to Arabs, who took them to the coast to sell them to Europeans.

Slavery in the North was soon found to be unprofitable. Since a veneer of Judeo-Christian religion forbids one from killing innocents slaves were set free, so you did not have to take care of them in old age!

Slavery would have died out from the same cause in the South except for the invention of the Cotton Gin in 1774. Why cotton? Because wool underwear cannot be washed, it shrinks and thus disease limited the growth of population. Cotton underwear can be washed. Once cotton underwear was introduced the population of Europe doubled in a few decades. The mills of England, France and New England could not get enough of cotton to meet the demand. That is why England and France almost sided with the South in the Civil War. That is why New York City sided with the South. White labor did not want competition with Slave labor.

Since slavery was against the "Christian" principles of most White Americans its justification was built upon the canard tht Blacks were racially inferior and so slavery was actually beneficial to them because it gave them employment and a chance to be cultivated even as they were denied the possibility of education!

The point that I am making is that no one group is innocent, all are guilty. Some profited and others were exploited but all were the product of the forces that shaped how this country came into being.

What makes us different is that those who shaped the foundation of this country flawed as they were, were also followers of the great thinkers of the "Enlightenment." Voltaire, Rousseau, Locke. **The founding documents of this country were based upon ideals that went beyond the reality of the existence of the men who wrote them.** And that is the key to what we should be celebrating.

Browning wrote: "Man's reach should exceed his grasp or what's a heaven for?"

The American experience has been the clash between those who wish to preserve the culture of the past that produced these documents and those who wish to bring to fruition the promise that these founding documents hold for an ever more just and understanding appreciation of the worth and possibility of each and every inhabitant of this great land.

As an American of Jewish faith that looks forward to the halcyon days of a Messiah, I cast my lot with those who see the promise of the future to be a validation of the ideals of the past not an attempt to turn the clock back to the realia of a life of past experience.

We celebrate the fourth of July!

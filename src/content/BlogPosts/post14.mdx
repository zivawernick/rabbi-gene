The Path to Peace and Freedom

It is a prerequisite for any person or people to turn a critical eye to their own beliefs and actions.  The Jewish people above all should know this.  The season of repentance that stretches from the month of Ellul through Hosannah Rabbah is rehearsed every day three times in the daily prayers as we ask for forgiveness.  One cannot ask for forgiveness without the admission that we have failed in our actions to live up to the standard dictated by our tradition from Biblical prophets to rabbis and sages of old and today.

While the charges of Genocide and other canards against Israel are false they cannot blind us to acts that were committed both in Gaza and the West Bank that  rank as pure evil committed without fear of retribution from a government that has turned not only  a blind eye to such abuses put loudly trumpets support from within the very recesses of power by members of the inner cabinet. 

A rabbinate that is more interested in asserting its power to lay claim to stipend from the public funds and laying a mantle of false spirituality over draft-dodging is a poor replica of the great sages of our past.  They speak the words of Hillel and in the same breath acquiesce to the deeds of Amalek!

Yes, I understand that an Israeli public having suffered two intifadas and the horror of October 7th is loath to include Palestinians into the formation of a new government.

But truth, justice and common sense must prevail for Israel to survive as a Jewish State which must mean living by the ethical values on which our national union is based.

Only with the admission of Arab Palestinians into the halls of power who are more than willing to be part of the national fabric can Israel establish the peace that will provide for all of its inhabitants.  Not only will that bring tranquility to the land but a normalcy of peace with all who are neighbors of Israel and remove the false basis from the threat of Iran.

Logic demands a terrible price from those who have been threatened.  I understand the reluctance that drives too many Israelis away from the propositions I am enunciating.

But for each and everyone of my brothers and sisters in Israel please listen to what I have proposed.  It is said by someone who for over sixty years raised millions for Israel, someone who has led marches to Washington on more than one occasion.  Someone for whom the existence of Israel is dearer than life itself.

Someone who has a grandchild in the land and a son who stands tall in defense of the land and its people.  Someone who has taught dozens of young men and women who have gone on to join the IDF.

The future can be won in the words of Herzl

“If you will it, it is not a dream!”

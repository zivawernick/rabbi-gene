On Tuesday, October 27th Israelis will vote not only for an election but for the future of the Jewish State.

I appreciate that after the Intifadas and October 7th most Jewish Israelis do not have warm and fuzzy feelings for Arabs either within or outside the borders of their country.

I for one reject the charge of Genocide leveled at the recent war in Gaza. That is not to admit that serious wrongs have been done in the past and continue into the present by Jews against Arabs and Arabs against Jews.

Both sides can enumerate too long a list of wrongdoings by the other.

However, I would like to remind my Jewish voters in Israel of Menahem Begin.

Menahem Begin began his career as far right as you can go. But as Prime Minister of Israel he talked of "Civil Courage." That was demonstrated when he gave back the Sinai to Egypt and made peace with Anwar Sadat. Sadat paid with his life for that courageous step. But the peace with Egypt has held now for so many years.

It is time for Jewish Israelis to take up the cause of "Civil Courage" and not let Arab parties just be silent partners in a new government but be an intrinsic part thereof.

The Arab citizens of Israel have nothing to gain from the forces outside Israel that wish to destroy it. Not only Jews but Arabs and Asians were massacred on October 7th.

There is much to be done to improve the life of the Arab citizens of Israel. But so many are productive as doctors, nurses and serve in the police and in the IDF.

Arabic should be returned as a second national language of the State.

The history of Jews in Moslem lands was not without incident and murder, but the overall experience was better than that in Christian Europe and developed a distinct rich addition to Jewish life in literature, law and most of all philosophy.

The greatest rabbis after the Talmud period include Maimonides, Abraham ibn Ezra and Joseph Karo all influenced by Arabic civilization.

Our Liturgy is enhanced by the poetry of Yehudah HaLevi and that poetry is in the meter of Arabic poetry!

It is time to have the past redeem the present so that a future may be crafted that brings the only real security that comes with peace and understanding.

The Sad Reality

George Santyana is quoted” Those who cannot remember the past are condemned to repeat it.”

The sad reality of Jewish existence is that whenever a Jewish National State existed it was destroyed by the overreach of a leadership that did not understand the limitations of their power.

That happened in 722 BCE with the fall of the Northern Israel monarchy and the subsequent exile of the population to Assyria.

It happened in 586 BCE with the fall of the Judean monarchy and the subsequent exile of the people to Babylonia.

It happened in 70 CE with the defeat of the rebels against Rome and the burning of the Temple.

It happened in 135 CE with the defeat of Bar Kochba in the rebellion against the Romans.

The sad reality of Jewish existence is having a national state where the levers of power are seized by persons who either by fanatical zeal or misplaced appreciation of the reality of power politics bring about disaster to the people and the homeland.

The truth of history is that it is only in democracies that are based upon the rational philosophies of the “Enlightenment” do Jews flourish.

That is true of the United States, France, England, Weimar and present Germany.

Having a Muslim on the Supreme court, having Muslims and others being part of the Knesset, in the IDF, in the police force as well as their complete integration into the society is the desiderata for Jewish life to flourish once again in its Homeland.

To overcome alien thoughts of Jewish superiority, terrible deeds done by a rabble that does not know history or the great ideas that have made Judaism a light unto the world is a tall order.

But as Santyana wrote so I fear that if those who are now in control of the levers of power in Israel then my worst fears for its future might be realized.

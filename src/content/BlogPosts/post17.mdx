We live in dangerous times both here and abroad.

Peace seems beyond reach and compromise with evil is too evident.

The last words of the Haftarah from the prophet Micah for this Shabbat still echo as a call to all humanity to seek what is good for each and everyone of us:

“It has been told to you Adam (as the first human he stands for all humanity not just Jews but everyone) what is good and what God desires of you:  But to do justice, love of abounding goodness to all and every, and to walk humbly with your God.”

There is no other way if we and that means each and everyone of us follows the path that leads to peace and accomplishment.  The other ways are of death and destruction!

Shabbat Shalom

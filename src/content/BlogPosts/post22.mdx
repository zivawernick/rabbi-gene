The length of your Fringes does not indicate the depth of your moral character!

It is time for all Jews and especially for Israelis to call what the religious establishment in Israel is-nothing but a scam for men to shirk their responsibilities to family and community. For their leadership to live as overlords of drones who do their bidding while they enjoy comfort and prestige.

If the non-orthodox Israelis had any true discernment, they would know that they had more in common with the non-Jewish elements of the state who willingly would share if given more of the opportunity to be integrated into the fabric of Israeli society. As of now you have non-Jewish Israelis who are in the professions caring for the health and well being of all. Non-Jewish Israelis who are in the police force and the IDF.

The cowards who can take the time away from their so-called studies of sacred texts to battle police and assault the homes of judges are unwilling to share the burden of protecting even themselves and their families.

If one of the so-called sages of these draft-dodgers threatened that if the law was enforced that they would emigrate-Well then let them do so!

Let them see where their grifting would be tolerated!

I speak as someone who has studied both Talmuds, Midrashim and Perushim.

After I was ordained as a rabbi I volunteered for the United States Air Force. That was the obligation that the Seminary I graduated from expected of its students.

And I had the great good fortune to study under the greatest authorities on Jewish culture and history that was ever assembled in one place.

Cowardice masked as piety is not a Jewish virtue!

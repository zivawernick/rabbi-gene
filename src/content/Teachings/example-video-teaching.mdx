So Balak is afraid. And I want us to notice what a frightened king does in this
story — he doesn't build walls, he doesn't send messengers to negotiate. He
sends for Balaam, a man whose whole profession is words.

And the donkey sees what the prophet cannot. Three times she swerves, three
times he strikes her. The seer — that is what Balaam is called, a seer — is the
only one in the scene who cannot see.
